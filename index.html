Pdime Restaurant celebrates the rich heritage of African soups and swallow. Our dishes are prepared using traditional recipes, fresh ingredients, and carefully selected spices to deliver rich, satisfying flavors that feel like home.

Whether you love Egusi, Afang, Ogbono, or Okra, every bowl is crafted to bring you the authentic taste of African cuisine — warm, nourishing, and unforgettable.

Our Offerings

Our Legendary Soups

Our soups are prepared fresh with traditional recipes and premium ingredients to deliver authentic African flavor.

Rich Egusi Soup

Ground melon seeds slow-cooked in a rich palm oil base with assorted meats, fish, and seasonal vegetables.

Most Popular
Traditional Ogbono Soup

Wild mango seeds with a naturally thick, draw consistency, cooked with assorted meats and smoked fish.

Customer Favourite
Fresh Okra Soup

Freshly sliced okra in a light, flavorful broth with choice proteins, and a silky, satisfying draw.

Light & Fresh
Authentic Afang Soup

A prized Cross River delicacy made with Afang leaves, water leaves, and an abundance of assorted seafood and meats.

Premium
Classic Bitterleaf Soup

Rich and hearty Igbo-style bitter leaf soup with cocoyam thickener, palm oil, and assorted meats.

Traditional
Seafood Okra Soup

Luscious okra loaded with crab, shrimp, periwinkle, and fish — a coastal feast in every bowl.

Seafood Special
Native White Soup (Ofe Nsala)

A delicate, peppery broth thickened with yam, featuring goat meat or catfish with fragrant utazi leaves.

Anambra Classic
Spicy Fisherman Soup

A fiery, aromatic Delta-style soup bursting with fresh fish, spicy scotch bonnet, and bold earthy spices.

Spicy
Oha Soup

Tender Oha leaves simmered in a thick cocoyam broth with assorted meats and crayfish. Subtly aromatic.

Seasonal Leaves
Banga Soup

Fresh palm nut extract simmered with fragrant spices, dried fish, and assorted meats. Rich, deep, and soul-warming.

Delta Special
Vegetable Soup (Edikang Ikong)

Ugwu and water leaves packed with assorted meats, periwinkle, and crayfish. Nutritious and deeply flavored.
